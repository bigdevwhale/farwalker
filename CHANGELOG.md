### Added
- The world from Natural Earth: 195 states and 47 other lands, 5,500 towns, roads that keep to land, sea lanes, real bridges and tunnels, 22 ends of the earth.
- Walking by the phone's step counter, in the background too; a coin per km walked.
- Trains (8 km a coin, from anywhere on the road to a station ahead) and ships (15 km a coin), both running on the clock.
- The engraved atlas, the passport with a stamp for every country, the journal of nights, the goal search.
- Local time and real weather where the traveller is; news of the road as notifications.
